Everyone may find different aspects of this tool useful to them. Here is my personal workflow:

  1. Choose the most illuminating metric by examining the differentiation expressed in the journal metric–prospect plots.
  2. Go to the Table tab and click E (= Expectation) to sort journals by expected impact.
  3. Work down the ordered list, adding each reasonable suggestion to your target journal list — stopping when you reach two lower-tier journals you are highly confident will accept the paper.
  4. Add any journals you think are missing, then reorder by how excited you would be to have the paper accepted there.

Personal priorities

  • Journals owned by scientific societies
  • Open-access journals
  • Journals with reasonable author fees

— Jeffrey Townsend
